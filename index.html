DeviceMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

DeviceMate ("the app") is developed by Sheffer Fernandes. It connects two of your own phones over Bluetooth: one phone with your SIM card (the "Caller Phone") and one mounted on your motorcycle's handlebar (the "Display Phone"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what happens to it.

In short: DeviceMate has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information to us or to anyone else. Information only travels over Bluetooth between the two phones you set up.

Information the app uses

Notifications and full-screen alertsTo show incoming calls on your Display Phone, including over the lock screen, and to keep the Bluetooth connection running.

How information is shared

Caller details, your Caller Phone's battery level, and your Answer, Decline and Open Maps commands are sent only over a direct Bluetooth connection between your Caller Phone and the Display Phone you choose. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party, and they are not sold. The app has no internet access.

What is stored, and for how long

The app saves only these settings on your phone:

  • which role the phone plays (Caller or Display),
  • the Bluetooth address of the Display Phone you selected,
  • whether the link is switched on, and
  • whether you have seen the tutorial.

Caller details and battery levels are not saved. They are kept in memory only while they are being shown. The app's debug log is also kept in memory only, and is cleared when you stop the link or the app is closed. App data is excluded from Android's cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Other apps

"Open Maps on Display Phone" opens Google Maps on your Display Phone, and "Wi-Fi Hotspot" opens your phone's settings. Information you enter in those apps is handled under their own privacy policies, such as Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can turn off any permission at any time in your phone's Settings. Contacts and the call screening role are optional; without them, the app still shows incoming calls, but may show only the number and the Decline button won't work.

Children

DeviceMate is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
